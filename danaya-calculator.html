Question: How much rice and dhal is needed for 100 people in Sri Lanka?
Answer: For roughly 100 guests on a Sri Lankan rice and curry spread, plan for around 13.5 to 15 kg of raw rice (the calculator adds a 10% serving buffer) and about 5 kg of red dhal (parippu), adjusted for whether you serve one or multiple rice dishes.

Question: How accurate is the estimated LKR budget on TopGoviya.lk?
Answer: The budget is an approximate estimate calculated from ingredient quantities and typical market rates. Actual spending varies by district, wholesale vs. retail buying, dish count, and daily price fluctuations.
