Ceylon cinnamon vs cassia

Ceylon cinnamon (Cinnamomum verum, "true cinnamon") is the cinnamon grown in Sri Lanka. Its quills are made of many thin, paper-like layers rolled together, light tan in colour, with a mild, sweet aroma.

Cassia (Chinese, Indonesian or Vietnamese cinnamon) is a different species. Its bark is a single thick, hard layer, darker and stronger tasting, and it contains much more coumarin than Ceylon cinnamon. That is why many buyers specifically look for Ceylon cinnamon.

Ceylon cinnamon is graded mainly by quill thickness: Alba is the finest, followed by the Continental (C), Mexican (M) and Hamburg (H) groups. The table above shows how the price falls as the quills get thicker.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Ceylon cinnamon price today?

For the week of 3 November 2026, C-5 averaged Rs. 5,716 per kg at the farm gate in Sri Lanka (about US$ 17.18 per kg), according to the Department of Export Agriculture.

Is this the export (FOB) price?

No. These are farm gate prices — what growers receive. Export (FOB) prices are higher because they include processing, grading, cleaning, fumigation, packing, transport and the exporter's margin.

What were the highest and lowest prices since 2023?

C-5 national average: highest Rs. 5,730 (15 September 2026), lowest Rs. 3,071 (14 November 2023).

How are the US$ prices calculated?

US$ prices use the Central Bank of Sri Lanka indicative USD/LKR spot rate of Rs. 332.61 (30 October 2026), for reference only.

Where does the data come from, and how often is it updated?

From the Department of Export Agriculture (DEA), Sri Lanka, weekly producers' (farm gate) price report. This page updates automatically when DEA publishes a new week. TopGoviya.lk is independent and does not buy or sell spices.
