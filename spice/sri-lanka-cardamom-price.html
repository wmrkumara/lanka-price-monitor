About Sri Lankan cardamom grades

The Department of Export Agriculture (DEA) reports green cardamom from Sri Lankan growers in several grades. LG is the top grade, followed by LLG1, LLG2 and LB, which sell at lower prices. The table above shows the weekly national average for each grade.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Sri Lankan cardamom price today?

For the week of 17 November 2026, LG averaged Rs. 14,667 per kg at the farm gate in Sri Lanka (about US$ 44.08 per kg), according to the Department of Export Agriculture.

Is this the export (FOB) price?

No. These are farm gate prices — what growers receive. Export (FOB) prices are higher because they include processing, grading, cleaning, fumigation, packing, transport and the exporter's margin.

What were the highest and lowest prices since 2023?

LG national average: highest Rs. 17,333 (11 February 2025), lowest Rs. 6,666 (31 January 2023).

How are the US$ prices calculated?

US$ prices use the Central Bank of Sri Lanka indicative USD/LKR spot rate of Rs. 332.74 (17 November 2026), for reference only.

Where does the data come from, and how often is it updated?

From the Department of Export Agriculture (DEA), Sri Lanka, weekly producers' (farm gate) price report. This page is updated each week when DEA publishes a new report. TopGoviya.lk is independent and does not buy or sell spices.
