Frequently asked questions

What is the Sri Lankan ginger price today?

For the week of 25 November 2026, Ginger averaged Rs. 497 per kg at the farm gate in Sri Lanka (about US$ 1.50 per kg), according to the Department of Export Agriculture.

Is this the export (FOB) price?

No. These are farm gate prices — what growers receive. Export (FOB) prices are higher because they include processing, grading, cleaning, fumigation, packing, transport and the exporter's margin.

What were the highest and lowest prices since 2023?

Ginger national average: highest Rs. 1,912 (30 July 2024), lowest Rs. 158 (7 February 2023).

How are the US$ prices calculated?

US$ prices use the Central Bank of Sri Lanka indicative USD/LKR spot rate of Rs. 332.55 (25 November 2026), for reference only.

Where does the data come from, and how often is it updated?

From the Department of Export Agriculture (DEA), Sri Lanka, weekly producers' (farm gate) price report. This page is updated each week when DEA publishes a new report. TopGoviya.lk is independent and does not buy or sell spices.
