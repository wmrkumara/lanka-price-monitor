In the DEA report for the week of 29 September 2026, the national average farm gate price of Ginger was Rs. 791 per kg (highest Rs. 1,000). That is down from Rs. 886 in the previous DEA report (week of 15 September 2026) (▼ -10.7%). A year earlier (30 September 2025) it was Rs. 1,337.
