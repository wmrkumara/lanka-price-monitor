In the DEA report for the week of 10 November 2026, the national average farm gate price of Ginger was Rs. 486 per kg (highest Rs. 750). That is down from Rs. 580 in the previous DEA report (week of 3 November 2026) (▼ -16.2%). A year earlier (11 November 2025) it was Rs. 985.
