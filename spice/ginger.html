In the DEA report for the week of 3 November 2026, the national average farm gate price of Ginger was Rs. 580 per kg (highest Rs. 930). That is down from Rs. 629 in the previous DEA report (week of 27 October 2026) (▼ -7.7%). A year earlier (4 November 2025) it was Rs. 1,011.
