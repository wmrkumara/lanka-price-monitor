About Sri Lankan pepper grades

DEA reports Sri Lankan black pepper in two grades. GR-1 is the higher grade (heavier, denser, cleaner berries); GR-2 is the next grade. White pepper is made by removing the outer skin, so it sells for more per kg.

Export buyers often also specify bulk density (for example 550 g/L) and moisture. Our spice price guide explains these terms.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Sri Lankan black pepper price today?

For the week of 3 November 2026, GR-1 black pepper averaged Rs. 2,065 per kg at the farm gate in Sri Lanka (about US$ 6.21 per kg), according to the Department of Export Agriculture.

Is this the export (FOB) price?

No. These are farm gate prices — what growers receive. Export (FOB) prices are higher because they include processing, grading, cleaning, fumigation, packing, transport and the exporter's margin.

What were the highest and lowest prices since 2023?

GR-1 black pepper national average: highest Rs. 2,120 (29 April 2025), lowest Rs. 1,322 (14 March 2023).

How are the US$ prices calculated?

US$ prices use the Central Bank of Sri Lanka indicative USD/LKR spot rate of Rs. 332.61 (30 October 2026), for reference only.

Where does the data come from, and how often is it updated?

From the Department of Export Agriculture (DEA), Sri Lanka, weekly producers' (farm gate) price report. This page updates automatically when DEA publishes a new week. TopGoviya.lk is independent and does not buy or sell spices.
