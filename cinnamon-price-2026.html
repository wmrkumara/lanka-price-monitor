🔍 Why Is Cinnamon Price Rising?

1. Global Demand for Ceylon Cinnamon

Ceylon Cinnamon (Cinnamomum verum) from Sri Lanka is recognized as the world's finest cinnamon variety. Growing health consciousness globally has driven demand from Europe, USA and India, pushing farm gate prices higher.

2. Currency Stabilization

After the 2022-2023 economic crisis, Sri Lanka's rupee stabilization meant exporters could offer better prices to farmers while maintaining export competitiveness.

3. Supply Constraints

The 2022 fertilizer ban and fuel shortages disrupted cinnamon cultivation. Reduced supply from 2023-2024 combined with steady demand has driven sustained price increases.

C-5 Cinnamon Price Sri Lanka 2026

The C-5 grade is the most widely traded cinnamon grade in Sri Lanka. As of September 2026, the C-5 cinnamon national average farm gate price is Rs. 5,730/kg, with the highest price of Rs. 6,000/kg available in Colombo district. C-5 cinnamon price has risen steadily from Rs. 3,071 in 2023 — a 86% increase in three years.

Alba Cinnamon Price Sri Lanka 2026

Alba is the premium Ceylon cinnamon grade with the thinnest quills. The Alba cinnamon national average price is Rs. 6,944/kg, with the highest price reaching Rs. 7,400/kg in Matara district. Alba commands a significant premium over C-5 grade and is most sought after by international buyers for its superior quality.
