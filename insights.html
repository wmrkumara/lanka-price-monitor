Common Questions

🤔 Frequently Asked Questions

What is the current vegetable price in Sri Lanka?

As of June 12, 2026 (Central Bank of Sri Lanka data): Tomato Rs.455/kg, Lime Rs.418/kg, Brinjal Rs.230/kg, Beans Rs.455/kg, Green Chilli Rs.545/kg at Dambulla Economic Centre. Prices updated daily at 5:00 PM Sri Lanka time on topgoviya.lk.

Why is the fish price rising in Sri Lanka?

Fish prices in Sri Lanka have been rising continuously since 2024. Linna fish has increased +55.7%, Hurulla +37.7%, and Balaya +33.2% over 2 years. Fuel costs, weather patterns, and increased demand are primary factors.

Is Dambulla market cheaper than Pettah?

Yes. Dambulla Economic Centre wholesale prices are consistently lower than Pettah retail prices. The average gap is 30-87% depending on the commodity. This creates transport and trading opportunities. Use the Gap Chart on TopGoviya.lk to see real-time differences.

What is the rice price in Sri Lanka in 2026?

Current Sri Lanka rice prices (June 2026): Samba Rs.245/kg, Nadu Rs.235/kg, Kekulu Rs.225/kg, Ponni Samba (Imported) Rs.240/kg. Rice prices have been stable — less than 17% variation over 3 years.
