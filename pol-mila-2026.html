Since 2023 the price of a coconut has roughly doubled. The biggest jump came in 2025 (+52%). Prices fell back in the first 9 months of 2026 (July 2026 average Rs. 112) but are now rising again. The lowest monthly average in 4 years was Rs. 70 (August 2023) and the highest Rs. 161 (October 2025).

⚡ +41% in 4 months

From a July 2026 monthly average of Rs. 112 to Rs. 158 in November (to the 20th), the Dambulla coconut price rose for 4 months in a row (+Rs. 46, ~41%). The CBSL report of 4 November gives limited supply from the Kurunegala area as one reason.

🗓️ Which months have the highest coconut prices?

Over the last 3 full years, coconut prices were above the annual average from October to December (December highest, ~+12%), and lowest in July and August (July ~-12%).

⚠️ This is only the average pattern of the last 3 years, not a rule. Prices depend on weather, harvests and industry demand.

According to the CDA report of 18 November, the farm gate price was Rs. 110,000–125,000 per 1,000 nuts (Rs. 110–125 a nut; the chart shows the middle). For a nut sold for Rs. 200 in Colombo, the farmer gets a little over half. There are transport, husking, losses and trader costs too, so this gap is not all profit.

Auction prices move on their own: the average price at the Colombo coconut auction fell from Rs. 137,848 per 1,000 nuts on 4 November to Rs. 113,070 on 18 November (~-18%), while shop prices stayed around Rs. 160. At the same time last year (20 Nov 2025) the auction price was Rs. 108,778.

What is the coconut price today in Sri Lanka?

According to the CBSL report of 20 November 2026, one coconut cost Rs. 163 in Dambulla, Rs. 175 in Pettah and Rs. 200 in Narahenpita. The latest price is on the retail price page, updated daily.
