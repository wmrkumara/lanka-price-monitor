About these prices

What is the Dambulla market price? It is the daily wholesale price range (minimum – maximum) published by the Dambulla Dedicated Economic Centre, one of Sri Lanka's main wholesale vegetable markets. Average = (min + max) ÷ 2.

Are these retail prices? No. Shop (retail) prices are higher because of transport and other costs. For retail prices see CBSL retail prices.

How often is it updated? Every day, including weekends. The date above is the date of the latest report.
