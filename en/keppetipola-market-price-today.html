About these prices

What is the Keppetipola market price? It is the daily wholesale price range (minimum – maximum) published by the Keppetipola Dedicated Economic Centre, Sri Lanka's main up-country vegetable wholesale market. Average = (min + max) ÷ 2.

Are these retail prices? No. Shop (retail) prices are higher because of transport and other costs. For retail prices see CBSL retail prices.

How often is it updated? On most working days. The date above is the date of the latest report.
